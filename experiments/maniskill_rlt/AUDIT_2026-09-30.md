## Implementation and contract

`rlt_memory_encoder.py::response_features` estimates per-joint command/response slopes and excitation support from completed records. It masks invalid records and unexecuted action suffixes, then zero-pads to the existing 64D interface. It adds no parameters, estimates no contact force, and does not label low response as collision. Environment construction checks that normalized arm-controller bounds match `joint_delta_scale`.

The new overlay is `experiment/rlt_memory_response`. Old checkpoints receive defaults for new fields; unknown or incompatible fields remain errors.

## Follow-up: Old experience can become misleading

`response_features(..., record_weights=...)` supports finite nonnegative past-record weights; its default remains numerically identical. The new CPU diagnostic `toolkits.rlt.probe_response_shift` compares uniform history, three fixed forgetting half-lives and an adaptive reset. Reset occurs only after two observed prediction errors exceed 0.015 joint-change units; it retains the latest two records for the next decision. This rule is diagnostic-only, not enabled in the online actor.

Six paired seeds cover stationary, gain-drop and gain-rise synthetic plants: 90 streams, not 90 trained models or robot trials. Fixed rapid forgetting improves adaptation but raises stationary late MSE from 4.65e-6 to 1.12e-5. The adaptive follow-up preserves stationary MSE at 4.65e-6 and reduces first-eight-step MSE after the drop from 6.96e-4 to 3.34e-4 (rise: 7.36e-4 to 3.58e-4). All changed streams reset after observing tick 61; none of the stationary streams reset. Excitation support is approximately 0.993 at the change, demonstrating why support must not be called confidence. These idealized discontinuities do not validate friction/contact detection or deployment thresholds.

The adaptive rule was added after inspecting the fixed-decay comparison; it is an exploratory follow-up, not an independent confirmation. The earlier 72-stream report is retained. Reproduce with a fresh directory:

```bash
CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES='' OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 python -m toolkits.rlt.probe_response_shift \\
  --output "$RLT_STORAGE/research/zeva_retention_new"
```
